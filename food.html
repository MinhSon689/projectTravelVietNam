Travel in Vietnam

Vietnam is a country blessed with magnificent natural wonders, famous landscapes, unique cuisine, and beautiful traditional costumes rich in cultural identity. From breathtaking bays and ancient towns to flavorful dishes and elegant traditional clothing, each aspect reflects the charm and spirit of the Vietnamese people. Let us explore and admire the timeless beauty of Vietnam together.

1. Pho

    It’s impossible to talk about Vietnamese cuisine without mentioning pho, a traditional dish considered a symbol of the country’s food culture. In particular, Hanoi pho stands out with its clear, light, and naturally sweet broth made from slow-simmered bones, along with the fragrant aroma of star anise, cinnamon, and cardamom. Just a squeeze of lime and a few slices of chili are enough to create a perfectly flavorful bowl of pho.

2. Banh Mi

    Vietnamese banh mi is a perfect combination of Western culture and local ingredients. The baguette has a crispy crust and a soft, fluffy inside, and is filled with various ingredients such as Vietnamese pork sausage, grilled meat, pâté, fried egg, fresh vegetables, cucumber, and flavorful sauces.

3. Bun Cha Ha Noi

    Bun cha Hanoi is a famous traditional dish from Hanoi. It consists of grilled pork served with rice noodles, fresh herbs, and a sweet-sour fish sauce dipping broth. The smoky flavor of the grilled meat combined with the fresh noodles and herbs creates a very balanced and delicious taste.

4. Xoi

    Vietnamese sticky rice (xoi) is a simple yet special dish, commonly enjoyed in both everyday meals and festivals. From xoi xeo, xoi peanuts, and xoi gac to five-color sticky rice, each type has its own meaning and unique flavor. Sticky, fragrant, and diverse in colors and tastes, xoi is not only a food but also a symbol of fullness and prosperity in Vietnamese culture.

5. Com Tam

    Com tam Saigon is a signature dish of Southern Vietnam, loved for its rustic yet rich flavor. It features broken rice grains cooked until soft, served with grilled pork ribs, fried egg, steamed egg meatloaf, and pickled vegetables.
